01 / about

Engineering that holds up in the real world.

I enjoy backend problems that don't fit in a single request: slow third-party APIs, millions of records, at-least-once delivery and race conditions. I solve them with queues and workers, idempotency, retries with backoff and well-written queries.

At CodeLeap (London, remote, working in English) I wrote the AWS infrastructure in Terraform and moved an LLM-based code grader to Lambda + SQS. At Dizevolv I built event-driven, multi-tenant SaaS products from scratch, with LLMs and RAG. At the São Luís City Hall I build systems that replaced paper processes with legally valid digital workflows.

When the product needs it, I build the frontend in React and TypeScript, and the mobile app in React Native.
